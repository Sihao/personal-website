    text: "I am currently a Postdoctoral Scientist in the Hudspeth Lab at Rockefeller University in New York City. My research interests include astrocyte and neuronal population activity and cochlear mechanics. Before this, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the Vaziri Lab at Rockefeller University, where I studied large-scale neural dynamics in vivo during sensory processing with multiphoton imaging. I completed my PhD at Imperial College London under the supervision of [Andriy Kozlov](https://kozlovlab.com), where I studied complex sound representation in the brain. My work investigated the receptive field properties of both astrocytes and neurons in the auditory cortex in response to natural stimuli."
